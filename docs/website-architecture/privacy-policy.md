# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 2, 2026

Tech Transpire ("Tech Transpire," "we," "us") is a trade name (DBA) of Akhani LLC, based in Dallas–Fort Worth, Texas. This policy explains what personal information we collect through **www.techtranspire.com** (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information we collect

**Information you give us through the contact form.**

| Information | Required? |
|---|---|
| Name | Yes |
| Company | Yes |
| Work email address | Yes |
| Phone number | No |
| Area of interest | Yes |
| Your message | Yes |
| How you heard about us | No |
| Your privacy consent, and the date and time you gave it | Yes |

Please do not include sensitive information, such as health information, financial account details or government ID numbers, in your message.

**Information you give us when booking a call.** If you book an intro call, you use a scheduling page operated by Microsoft (Microsoft Bookings). Microsoft collects the details you enter there, such as your name, email address and chosen time, on our behalf.

**Information you give us by email or phone.** If you contact us at info@techtranspire.com or +1 (979) 479-2779, we receive whatever you choose to send.

**Information collected automatically.** The Site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub records technical information about each visit, including your IP address, to deliver the Site and protect it from abuse. We do not receive or use these records.

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

## 2. How we use your information

| Purpose | Information used |
|---|---|
| Responding to your inquiry and arranging conversations | Contact form, booking, email and phone details |
| Recording your inquiry so we can follow up and keep a history of our conversations | Contact form details |
| Notifying our team that a new inquiry has arrived | Contact form details, sent by internal email |
| Understanding how people find us | "How you heard about us" |
| Keeping the contact form free of spam | Form timing and other anti-spam signals |
| Meeting legal obligations | As required |

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do **not** share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 3. Legal bases (if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom)

We process your inquiry details to take steps at your request before a possible contract, and for our legitimate interest in responding to and managing business inquiries.

## 4. Who we share information with

We use service providers that process information on our behalf and under our instructions:

| Provider | Role |
|---|---|
| Google LLC (Google Apps Script and Google Sheets) | Receives contact form submissions and stores them |
| Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft 365 and Microsoft Bookings) | Hosts our mailbox, delivers inquiry notifications to our team and runs intro-call scheduling |
| Zoho Corporation (Zoho CRM) | Stores inquiries we follow up on in our customer relationship management system |
| GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages) | Hosts the Site |

Tech Transpire and True Lean Solutions are both operated by Akhani LLC. If your inquiry is better served by the True Lean Solutions team, we may share it with them so they can respond.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or as part of a business transfer, such as a merger or sale of Akhani LLC or the Tech Transpire business.

## 5. Cookies

The Site does not set cookies. Services we link to, such as Microsoft Bookings and LinkedIn, may set their own cookies when you visit them, under their own policies.

## 6. Where information is processed

Our service providers process information in the United States and may process it in other countries where they operate.

## 7. How long we keep information

| Information | Retention |
|---|---|
| Inquiries that do not lead to an engagement | Deleted within 24 months of our last contact with you |
| Inquiries from clients | For the length of the relationship, plus any period required for legal, tax or accounting records |
| Submissions identified as spam | Deleted |
| Hosting records | Kept by GitHub under its own policies |

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to the accounts that hold inquiries. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## 9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing **info@techtranspire.com**. We will verify your request and respond within 45 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as data portability, the right to object to processing, the right to appeal our decision about your request, or the right to complain to a data protection authority.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

## 10. Children

The Site is intended for business audiences and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

## 11. Links to other sites

The Site links to other services, such as LinkedIn and Microsoft Bookings. Their own privacy policies govern how they handle your information.

## 12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. If the changes are significant, we will make that clear on the Site.
