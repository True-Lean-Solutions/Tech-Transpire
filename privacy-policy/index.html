We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access to the accounts that hold inquiries. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@techtranspire.com. We will verify your request and respond within 45 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as data portability, the right to object to processing, the right to appeal our decision about your request, or the right to complain to a data protection authority.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

10. Children

The Site is intended for business audiences and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.
